02 Remote

Everywhere else

The city pages exist because local searches are how a lot of businesses look for a developer, and because the trade in each place genuinely changes the brief. They are not a limit.

Work is remote-first. Briefs, feedback and handover happen over call and email, and that has been true for every project so far — including the ones for clients in Saudi Arabia, Poland and Nepal, none of whom have ever been in the same room as the person building their site.
